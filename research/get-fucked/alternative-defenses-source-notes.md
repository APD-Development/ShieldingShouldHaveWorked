# Alternative defenses: source checks during continuous caves replay

These are source deductions, not additional completed routes. Production code is unchanged. The original 1 maximum HP and lethal starvation rules remain in force.

* Mage Shield Battery uses `round(HT * .04 * charges)` (times 1.5 with two talent points). At HT=1, one talent point gives zero shielding for any ordinary 1–10 charge wand; two points give zero at 1–8 charges and one at 9–10. Staff charge cap is ten. This is an unusually weak defensive conversion, not a general Mage impossibility proof. Source: Wand.java targeting callback; MagesStaff.java updateWand.
* Backup Barrier is independent of HT: three/five shield immediately before the final charged zap. For a Mage it requires the wand in the staff, not an arbitrary loose inventory wand. This can protect against a zap-triggered skeleton explosion, but is a maximum-setting operation rather than an additive five-shield grant every cast. It still needs earned talent points and real recharges.
* Warlock Soul Eater's nutrition is independent of whether healing is useful. A marked enemy physically attacked by the hero restores hunger by effective damage times talent points / 3. Ally attacks first scale the restoration to `round(damage * .4 * SoulSiphonPoints / 3)`. A fully invested pair therefore yields roughly 40% of allied physical damage as nutrition, subject to rounding and remaining enemy HP/shields. Wand damage alone does not use this physical-defense hook. Soul marking itself is probabilistic (`1 - .92^(wandLevel*chargesUsed+1) + .07`). A mark, safe physical damage and talent acquisition must all be demonstrated in a real route.
* Living Earth can make an independent-HP ally; its guardian health is `16 + 8*wandLevel`, armor roll is level through `3+3*level` without Faith Is My Armor, and it attracts attacked enemies. Its pre-guardian Rock Armor only blocks half a hit, limited by reserve. It is not equivalent to fully shielding a one-HP hero. The staff's melee healing/armor proc belongs to Battlemage and cannot be combined with Warlock Soul Eater. A Warlock can still zap the owned wand to build/heal its guardian.
* Duelist Aggressive Barrier requires HP/HT <= .5. A normally alive one-HP hero is at 100%, so that condition fails. This removes that particular defense; it does not rule out weapon abilities, Monk defenses or other shields.
* Warden Shielding Dew caps shield at `round(HT*.2*points)`: zero with one/two points, one with three. A single dewdrop's `round(HT*.05)` is zero; ten or more simultaneous drops can yield one. This cannot be credited as the usual large dew reserve. Other Warden benefits remain separate questions.
* Berserker shield boost has an HT-independent base `8 + 2*armorLevel`, then a health-dependent multiplier. At normal 1/1 HP the multiplier is one. Rage gain divides incoming defenseProc damage by HT, which makes its threshold unusually quick at HT=1. Exact attack ordering, shield depletion, recovery, and Deathless Fury at zero HP still need a targeted legal action test; no Berserker full route is established here.

Source snapshots in caves-source-cache retain Talent, Wand, MagesStaff, Mob, SoulMark, WandOfLivingEarth, Dewdrop, MeleeWeapon, Berserk, Hero and their exact implementation. These checks favor investigating Warlock nutrition/guardian or Berserker alongside continued Paladin experiments; they do not establish their early game, bosses or ascension.

## Corrections relevant to the current route

Guiding Light can reapply Illuminated on later casts. Its callback unconditionally applies Illuminated and WasIlluminatedTracker to a surviving struck character. WasIlluminatedTracker is not a once-only gate in this callback. The compiled runtime's GuidingLight$1 bytecode was checked against this source. Each physical attack consumes Illuminated; a new paid cast can apply it again. This differs from Sunray's explicit permanent once-only blindness marker. Any earlier note conflating these two limits is superseded by this source/runtime check.

The current character has 15 strength and no owned strength potion. Floor 14's strength potion can either be drunk or consumed to craft Mastery; it cannot serve both purposes. Converting that potion into Mastery leaves strength at 15. Greatsword+0 with Mastery then requires 16; Greatsword+1 with Mastery requires 15. Understrength equipment is legally usable with penalties, but cannot be credited with qualified-weapon guaranteed accuracy. The uncollected upgrade scroll remains a real acquisition requirement for the proposed qualified Greatsword build.

Earthroot is another source-level corner case to test, not a credited defense in this route. Its stored armor is HT=1, but `Armor.absorb` chooses `min(damage, (scalingDepth+5)/2)` before testing whether the reserve is exhausted. It therefore appears to block up to nine points on its one final physical hit at depth 14, rather than capping that hit's block at the one-point stored reserve. Char.defenseProc calls this; direct damage such as a shaman bolt does not use that physical-defense hook. It does not remove the spinner's on-hit poison. No planted Earthroot action is part of the current completed witness.
