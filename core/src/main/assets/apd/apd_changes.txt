APD 4.0.8-apd
Added:
- Difficulty 1-5 (APD Settings, "Difficulty (new runs)"): 3 is the game exactly as it is. The other levels do not change gameplay yet (their balance is not decided); for now they play like 3 but your runs are recorded with the level you chose, as their own ruleset. A run keeps the difficulty it started with, and runs from before difficulty existed show it as unknown. UIMSD can filter and group runs by difficulty
- UIMSD reports: share or save a report of your summary, the table you are looking at, a single run, a group (a class, item kind, enemy...), two runs side by side, or the seed floors. Share it as text, or save it as Markdown, a web page (print it to PDF from any browser) or CSV. Reports keep the marks (? unknown, >= at least, ~ inferred) with a legend, say plainly which data is canonical (a seed replay, not recorded history), and never include hidden information of a run that is still going
- UIMSD seed floors: open a finished run, tap "Seed floors (canonical)" and see what every floor of its seed holds, including floors the run never reached: items with their true identity, where they lie, and creatures. Floors the run recorded always show the run's own log; every other row is labelled canonical (a replay of the seed under fixed reference rules) and marked ~, because play changes what later floors hold. A comparison table sets recorded against canonical floor by floor. Only for runs that are over, and only while no game is loaded
- UIMSD Home and Guide: UIMSD now opens on a Home page with your runs, wins, win rate, deepest floor, characters, most used gear, the enemies you meet and that ended your runs, recent runs and records, each with a button into the full tables (All tables still has everything). The Guide (button on Home, ? in the tables) explains datasets, rows, drill-down, filters, sorting, columns, the marks (? >= ~), sample size and coverage, and starts an interactive tour on the real screens, using your own runs or a small made-up sample that is deleted afterwards
- History export and import (APD Settings): save your whole run history as one ZIP file, anywhere you choose, and import it on another device or install. Importing only ever adds: nothing already there is replaced, every file is checked first, and a damaged or tampered archive is refused. A run that is not finished is exported without its hidden information. APD Settings also shows how much space the history takes, with a warning when it gets large. Nothing is ever deleted automatically
- UIMSD: the UIMSD button on the home screen now opens the statistics screen (also reachable from APD Settings). Browse Runs, Items (with Weapons, Armor, Rings, Trinkets and Artifacts views), Enemies, Deaths, Characters and Challenges; sort by tapping a column, add any number of filters, choose columns, group, and see how much of every number is actually known. Hidden information of a run that is still going is never shown
- APD's own rankings file is now copied into your APD history automatically when a run ends (and by the Import APD rankings button in APD Settings), so records can't fall out of its top 11 unseen. This only covers runs finished in APD, not the separately installed official game. Nothing is guessed: anything a record lacks stays unknown, and recorded runs remember which ranking entry they produced so they are never counted twice
- Run history now also records what the game generated for each level: every item with its true identity and where it was placed, every enemy, and how the level was built, kept strictly separate from what you could see
- Healing is now recorded exactly: how much each heal gave, and (kept as hidden information until the run is finished) what healed you. UIMSD shows Healed, the part that was only guessed, and for finished runs the healing by kind of source. Anything the game does not report is still noticed and marked as a guess, and runs recorded before this keep their guessed healing
- Run history now also records fights and health: attacks, damage (including what shielding absorbed), kills, experience, hunger, healing (marked as inferred) and deaths, keeping what you could see apart from hidden enemy details
- Run history now gives every item you meet a persistent identity (apd_uid) and records seeing, picking up, equipping, learning about and losing it, without ever recording what you could not have known yet
- APD Settings in the pause menu
- Exotic crystal conversion chance setting (0-80%, 10% steps)
- Custom APD title/home screen: stone ADHD PIXEL DUNGEON logo, animated green flames, new background and stone buttons. Two-column menu (Play / UIMSD, Badges / Journal, Options / Quit) with News / Changes / About below. Landscape puts the logo and menu side by side.
- UIMSD (Ultimate Infinite Mega Statistics Database) button on the home screen, replacing Rankings (opens the Rankings screen for now), with a very serious annotation pointing at it
- APD Settings button on the home screen (exotic chance, run history recording, self-test)
- APD INDEV version string (v4.0.8-apd-INDEV)
- APD News and APD Changes screens
- Sad Ghost now shows the real upgrade level of the gear it offers
- Cleric attempt counter and the achievement "FUCK CLERIC, THAT'S WHY" (10 attempts)
- Fresh installs go straight to the title menu
Changed:
- APD now requires Android 8.0 or newer (up from Android 5.0)
- Selecting Cleric and pressing Start now leads to a black screen until the app is closed
Fixed:
- Starvation damage now consumes shielding before HP (APD-0001). Shielding does not cure hunger.
Removed:
- Cleric as a playable class
- Support button on the home screen
